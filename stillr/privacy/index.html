Cifro

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 5, 2026

Overview

Cifro is designed with privacy as a core principle. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and what is never collected or shared.

Cifro does not operate servers, does not collect personal data, and does not share your information with third parties. All data lives on your device and, optionally, in your personal iCloud account.

iCloud Sync

Cifro uses iCloud to sync your data across your Apple devices. Your data is stored in your private iCloud account (CloudKit) and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Cifro never has access to your iCloud credentials or your data stored there — only you and your devices do.

iCloud sync is optional and can be disabled at any time from iOS Settings.

Camera — Receipt Scanning

Cifro requests camera access to scan receipts. Images captured during scanning are processed entirely on-device to extract transaction data. No photos are stored or transmitted outside your device.

Location — Vendor Detection

Cifro requests location access to identify the vendor or merchant associated with a transaction. Location data is used only at the moment of the request to perform a local lookup and is never stored, logged, or transmitted to any server.

Face ID / Biometric Authentication

Cifro uses Face ID (or Touch ID) to protect access to the app. Biometric data is managed entirely by iOS and the Secure Enclave — Cifro never has access to your biometric data. Authentication results (granted/denied) are the only signal the app receives.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any of the following on external servers:

  • Names, email addresses, or account information
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location history
  • Camera images or scanned receipts
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to third parties

Third-Party Services

Cifro does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Children's Privacy

Cifro does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Because we collect no personal data from any user, the app is safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
